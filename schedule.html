Good To Know

Before You Come In

  • Arrive 10–15 minutes early for your first class to fill out a quick waiver
  • Wear athletic clothing — a gi is provided for your first visit if you don't have one
  • No experience needed — Fundamentals class is built for first-timers
  • Bring a water bottle and a towel
