Good To Know

Questions, Answered.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Your first class at Washington Luis Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is free. Call or text (832) 908-1968, or use the contact form, and we will get you on the schedule.

Do I need any experience to start?

No. Adult Fundamentals meets Fridays at 6:30 PM and is built for first-timers, in a class made up of other beginners. Adult Striking on Saturdays is open to every level, with no Jiu-Jitsu experience required.

What should I wear and bring to my first class?

Wear athletic clothing. A gi is provided for your first visit if you do not have one. Bring a water bottle and a towel, and arrive 10 to 15 minutes early to fill out a quick waiver.

What ages do you teach?

We teach all ages: Little Ninjas for ages 4 to 6, Juniors Gi and No-Gi for ages 7 to 14, a Juniors Competition Team by coach recommendation, and adult classes in Gi, No-Gi, Striking and Women Only Jiu Jitsu.

Do you have a women-only class?

Yes. Adult Women Only Jiu Jitsu meets Fridays at 5:30 PM and is open to all experience levels.

Where are you located, and when is the front desk open?

We are at 4202 Center St, Deer Park, TX 77536. Front desk hours are Monday to Friday 4:30 PM to 8:30 PM and Saturday 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM. We are closed Sundays.

How much does membership cost?

Membership options are flexible: kids-only, adults-only, or full access. Call or text (832) 908-1968 and we will walk you through current pricing and the plan that fits your schedule.

Do you offer private lessons?

Yes. Private lessons are available by appointment for members and non-members alike. Call or text to set one up.
