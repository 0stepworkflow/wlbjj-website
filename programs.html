Nine ways to train at Washington Luis BJJ — pick the track that matches your age and goals, or move between them as a member.

Program 01

Adult Fundamentals

The on-ramp for adults brand new to Jiu-Jitsu. You'll learn the core positions, escapes, and submissions that everything else builds on — in a class made up entirely of other beginners, so there's no pressure to keep up with advanced students.

  • No experience or gi required to start
  • Meets Friday at 6:00 PM
  • Structured curriculum, not random classes

Program 02

Adult Gi & No-Gi

Once you've got the fundamentals down, this is where technique turns into game. Traditional gi classes focus on grips and control; No-Gi classes move faster and lean on speed and scrambles. Most members train both.

  • Gi: Mon, Wed & Fri at 12:00 PM, plus Mon & Wed at 7:15 PM
  • No-Gi: Tuesday & Thursday at 7:15 PM
  • Live rolling included in every session
