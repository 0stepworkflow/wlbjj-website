Professor Washington Luis

Jiu-Jitsu changed Professor Washington Luis's life as a young boy in Rio de Janeiro's favelas. He started training at age 10 at the Complexo do Lins social project under César “Casquinha” Guimarães, founder of Top Brother, and earned his black belt in 2011 before continuing his development at GF Team under Júlio César Pereira.

He moved to the U.S. in 2014, first coaching in Boston before leading the Paradigm Training Center – Southwest affiliate in Houston. Washington Luis Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu carries that same lineage into Deer Park — technique over ego, and a coaching staff that pays attention to every student on the mat, not just the advanced ones.
